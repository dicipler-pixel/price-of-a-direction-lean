# Provenance

`PriceOfDirection/Basic.lean` and the three files in `FalseControls/` were written for this
repository from *The Price of a Direction* (13 August 2026, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21918463),
Lemma 2.1 and Appendix B.


`PriceOfDirection/GapLaw.lean` and `FalseControls/tie_has_no_gap.lean` were added on 7 October 2026.
They formalize the gap law behind Lemma 2.1 and Proposition 5.2 (single directions lose
trackability while the shared subspace persists), prompted by its measurement on the NIST
dark-soliton condensate data (Fritsch et al., NIST mds2-2363): on every rank cut with relative gap
at least 0.10, 18 of 18, the measured subspace motion matched the coupling-over-gap prediction to
within 0.93–1.12.
